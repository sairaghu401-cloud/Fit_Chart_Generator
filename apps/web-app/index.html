About FitChart AI

The Problem

30–40% of online apparel purchases are returned, and most of that is caused by inconsistent sizing, static 2D measurement tables, and generic manufacturer charts that ignore fabric stretch, shrinkage, and body variation.

How It Works

A multi-agent AI pipeline reads garment images, fabric specs, and tech packs to auto-generate a dynamic size chart in seconds. A Bayesian fit model then matches shopper measurements against that chart in real time.

Benefits

  • Reduced size-related returns
  • Better customer experience
  • Personalized shopping, in milliseconds
